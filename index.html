About the project

A practical approach to student performance

The project uses academic information such as attendance, internal marks, assignments, quizzes and study hours to estimate student performance and provide useful study guidance.

It is designed as an academic support tool. Its predictions are estimates based on the available model and data, not guaranteed academic results.

How it works

Enter academic details such as CGPA, attendance and marks.
Add study hours, backlogs and other requested inputs.
Submit the information to the prediction dashboard.
Review the estimated result, analysis and study suggestions.
